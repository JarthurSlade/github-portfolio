About Me

My name is Joshua Arthur, and I am finishing my Computer Science degree at Lamar University in December 2026. I have always liked figuring out how things work, especially when it comes to computers. Over time, that interest has grown into wanting to work in software development or another technology-related field.

In my classes, I have worked with C++, Java, and Python. Some assignments have focused on algorithms and data structures, while others involved writing programs, finding mistakes, and figuring out why something was not working as expected. One example is my C++ sorting algorithms project, where I implemented insertion sort, merge sort, and radix sort. I like being able to compare different ways of solving the same problem and seeing how the code actually behaves.

I have also studied cybersecurity and computer systems. Topics such as network security, malware, and password security have helped me see how much goes into keeping technology working and protecting information. I am still exploring which area of computer science I want to focus on most, and I am interested in gaining more hands-on experience.

Outside the classroom, I have helped troubleshoot computer software and worked as a summer IT assistant at West Orange-Stark High School. That work included repairing Chromebooks, organizing equipment, and communicating with staff about technical problems. Those experiences showed me that technical work is not just about knowing the answer. It also involves listening carefully, asking questions, and being willing to work through a problem.

As I finish college, my goal is to find an entry-level position where I can keep learning and put my skills to use. I know there is still a lot for me to learn, but I enjoy improving through practice and working on projects that give me something real to show for the effort.
